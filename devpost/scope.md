# Dawa Equips Marketplace PoC

Proof-of-concept web marketplace for refurbished commercial appliances where a retailer can quickly publish and promote trusted stock, and visitors can confidently discover, buy, and track orders.

## The Unique Kernel
This is not just a listing site; it positions the retailer as a trusted quality equipment partner. The core proof is a clean, structured catalog plus deal visibility and order progress, so visitors feel confident buying second-hand equipment.

## Who It's For
- A refurbished-appliance retailer ("Dawa Coffee Machines" / "Dawa Equips") managing stock and promotions.
- Buyers looking for commercial appliances and spare parts who need clear product details, pricing context, and confidence in the purchase flow.

## The Core Loop
Retailer adds/imports products, promotes selected stock through flash/seasonal deals, and manages inventory/orders. Visitors browse with filters, add to cart, place orders, and track order progress. They return to discover updated stock and active deals.

## Inspiration & Identity
Jumia-inspired marketplace experience: promotion-forward homepage energy, clear category/search/filtering, and practical ecommerce navigation.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The learner wants to practice a stronger delivery process while shipping a realistic marketplace slice for a real refurbished-appliance business context.

## What "Working" Looks Like
In a short demo:
- Retailer imports/creates products (with 3+ images), sets inventory and pricing, and attaches products to flash/seasonal promotions.
- Visitor browses/filter-searches products, views product details, adds to cart, places an order, and sees order tracking/progress.
- Retailer can manage orders and product inventory from admin.

The "oh, that's cool" moment is seeing promoted products on the storefront and then completing a full browse-to-order-to-tracking flow tied to admin-managed stock.

## The POC Boundary
In scope now:
- Retailer: individual + CSV/bulk product import, minimum 3 product images, inventory and order management, promotion tagging (flash sale/seasonal deal).
- Visitor: browse products, filter by name/price/category/brand, use cart, place order, track order progress.

## Later
- SEO indexing optimization and rich search-result snippets.
- Live chat escalation (chatbot -> human), WhatsApp/call handoff.
- Payment integrations (including MPESA live checkout flow).
- Wishlist, recently viewed, full review/comment moderation features.
- AI-generated product-description completion.

## Explicitly Cut
- Full marketplace parity with Jumia in this phase: too broad for a 2-4 hour proof of concept.
- Real-time communications stack (live chat/agent routing): not required to prove the core trust-and-transaction loop.
- Production-grade payment rails in PoC: increases integration and compliance complexity beyond current scope.
